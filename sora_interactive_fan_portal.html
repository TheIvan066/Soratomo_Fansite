Lyrics & Grammar Study Workspace

Line 01 Vocabulary Highlighted
エイリアン わたしエイリアン
eirian watashi eirian
Alien, I am an alien
わたし (Watashi): I / myself (humble, Standard Japanese first-person pronoun).
Line 02 Grammar Concept
あなたの心を惑わせる
anata no kokoro wo madowaseru
Bewildering your heart
あなた (Anata): You (singular).
惑わせる (Madowaseru): To bewilder / to perplex. (Causative form of 惑う - madou).
Line 03 N4 Vocabulary
交信を試みる
koushin wo kokoromiru
Attempting to communicate
交信 (Koushin): Contact/telecommunications (often used for space/radio links).
試みる (Kokoromiru): To try / to attempt (Formal version of 試す - tamesu).
